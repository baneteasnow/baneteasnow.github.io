Color Palette Generator

Find a palette for your next project. Keep the colors you like, adjust each HEX code, or start with a book cover.

Enter a 3- or 6-digit HEX code. Six-digit codes update instantly; short codes apply when you leave the field. Press Esc to restore the current color.

Locked colors stay when you generate again. Press Space for a new palette.

Color Extractor

Extract five colors from a book cover or photograph. Your image stays on your device.

Applying replaces all five colors, including locked colors. Images with few colors may produce repeated swatches.
